e-Lab Komputer

A web app built for Madrasah Sains Qur'an Ar-Ridwan that replaces manual lab records with a clear digital workflow, from booking a schedule to printing official reports.

  • Conflict-free booking: free computers are calculated automatically for the chosen time slot
  • Role-based access for administrators, teachers, and staff, with admin-approved registration
  • Inventory management with bulk Excel import, plus blocking of classes or accounts with reasons
  • Official A4 reports with MTs/MA letterheads and QR-code digital signatures
  • Dashboard with usage trend charts, notifications, and admin notes

e-Absensi Madrasah

A multi-level madrasah system for teacher attendance, class attendance, teaching-hour (JTM) calculation, and late fines, so monthly reports no longer have to be compiled by hand.

  • Teacher check-in/out and per-class attendance with teaching journals and class notes
  • Automatic JTM and late-fine calculation, with rules the administrator can configure
  • Monthly schedules with conflict detection, a color-coded calendar, substitute and duty teachers
  • Official A4 recaps (JTM, attendance, class, journal, notes, fines) with QR-code signatures
  • Three roles (administrator, principal, teacher), login-attempt limits, and an activity log
